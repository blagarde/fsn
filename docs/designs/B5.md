# B5: Lead-cooled cermet

Seed branch from the [taxonomy](../framework/taxonomy.md#known-reactors-classified): it survives the exclusion rules and reaches T3–T4. Not yet analysed beyond this summary.

A refractory-metal matrix keeps fission products in at high temperature. W and Mo matrices cost neutrons (W fission-spectrum capture ~55 mb, see [tungsten](../materials/tungsten.md)); isotope tailoring may help.
