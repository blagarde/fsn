# A3: Al-U-Pu solution fuel

Uranium and plutonium dissolved in liquid aluminium form a single circulating fuel–coolant, so there is no fuel–coolant wall. This echoes Brookhaven's 1950s Liquid Metal Fuel Reactor design (uranium in bismuth). Heavy-metal density is lower (~6.5 gU/cm³ at UAl2 composition *(approx.)*) and aluminium softens the spectrum, so breeding margin is the first thing to check.

Background and comparison with sibling designs: [aluminium coolant](../studies/aluminium-coolant.md).
