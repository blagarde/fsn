# B3: Helium-cooled carbide or nitride

Seed branch from the [taxonomy](../framework/taxonomy.md#known-reactors-classified): it survives the exclusion rules and reaches T3–T4. Not yet analysed beyond this summary.

Direct Brayton cycle with no coolant phase change. The difficulty is removing decay heat passively after depressurisation, the classic gas-cooled fast reactor problem.
