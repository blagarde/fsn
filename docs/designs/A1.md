# A1: Solid nitride fuel, aluminium-cooled

T5 heat with solid fuel at near-atmospheric pressure. Aluminium needs no pressurisation up to its 2,470 °C boiling point, but it dissolves metallic cladding, so the cladding must be a diboride made with boron-11, or have an AlN outer layer. The main question is whether brittle ceramic cladding tolerates fission-gas pressure and swelling.

Background and comparison with sibling designs: [aluminium coolant](../studies/aluminium-coolant.md).
