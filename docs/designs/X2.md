# X2: Liquid U-Pu pool, CaF2 direct contact

Molten CaF2 droplets are injected at the bottom of the fuel pool, rise through the much denser fuel (~2.4 vs ~16 g/cm³), collect on top and carry heat to an external heat exchanger. No wall carries heat at 2,000 °C. Molten uranium and CaF2 slag already separate cleanly in metallothermic uranium production.

| Aspect | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Chemistry | CaF2 is more stable than uranium fluorides, so U does not reduce it |
| Separation | Fuel carry-over and emulsion stability unknown ([Q-022](../open-questions.md)) |
| Neutronics | Some fluorine moderation; reactivity worth of carrier fraction to compute ([Q-023](../open-questions.md)) |
| Activation | Clean (F-20, 11 s) |
