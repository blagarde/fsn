# X3: Liquid U-Pu pool, boiling calcium direct contact

Liquid calcium droplets boil inside the fuel pool; the vapour rises, separates and condenses outside, delivering heat at a constant temperature. Calcium is essentially immiscible with uranium (it reduces UF4 to uranium metal industrially).

| Aspect | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Pressure | ~9 bar at 2,000 °C (database, Clausius–Clapeyron extrapolation from JANAF; the doc's earlier ~11 bar estimate was close) |
| Heat transport | Large latent heat (~3.9 MJ/kg *(approx.)*), isothermal delivery at the condenser |
| Neutronics | Vapour voids change reactivity; must be computed ([Q-023](../open-questions.md)) |
| Activation | Near-clean: Ca-41 (long-lived, weak), Ca-45 from Ca-44 (2.1% abundance, database) |
