# B2: Lead-cooled nitride

Seed branch from the [taxonomy](../framework/taxonomy.md#known-reactors-classified): it survives the exclusion rules and reaches T3–T4. Not yet analysed beyond this summary.

High boiling point (1,749 °C) and almost no moderation. Lead corrodes steels and nickel alloys; steels need dissolved-oxygen control and stop around 550 °C. Mo, W and SiC are very weakly soluble, so a refractory or ceramic cladding is needed for T3.
