# X1: Liquid U-Pu pool, lead behind tungsten tubes

Liquid U–Pu metal fuel sits in a tungsten vessel; lead flows through tungsten tubes immersed in the pool. Lead barely dissolves tungsten, and the pressure difference across the wall is small (lead vapour pressure ~3 bar at 2,000 °C, [database-checked](../data/claims-check.md), extrapolated above Pb's boiling point).

| Aspect | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Pb–W compatibility | Excellent (W almost insoluble in Pb) |
| Fuel side | U–W solubility and thermal-gradient mass transfer unknown ([Q-020](../open-questions.md)) |
| Wall | Creep strength only a few MPa at 2,000 °C; must carry almost no pressure difference |
| Activation | Pb clean (Pb-209, 3.25 h); Pb-206/207 enrichment reduces Bi and Po-210 |
