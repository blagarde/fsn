# Uranium nitride

Dense (~13.5 gHM/cm³ *(approx.)*), conductive (~20 W/m·K) solid fuel used in seed branches B1–B3, B5–B6 and [A1](../designs/A1.md). Needs N-15 to avoid N-14 capture and C-14. Dissociates at high temperature under low nitrogen pressure.
