# D-0002: Direct industrial heat is in scope

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Status:** accepted
- **Affects:** [brief](../project/brief.md), [industrial heat](../studies/industrial-heat.md)

## Context

The reactor need not only produce electricity; heat could go directly to heavy industry, with serious co-location safety questions.

## Decision

Direct process heat is a first-class use case alongside electricity. Heat-mode targets use delivered exergy, and co-location hazards are modelled as design-basis transients.

## Consequences

Taxonomy axis 9 gained PH-x codes. Anchor use cases are still to choose ([Q-001](../open-questions.md)).
